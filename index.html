OneAnswerAI - Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 24, 2026

Developer: Valentino Paulon

Overview

OneAnswerAI is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your information.

Artificial Intelligence Disclosure

OneAnswerAI's answers are generated by third-party AI models (OpenAI, Google Gemini, Anthropic Claude), not by a human. AI-generated or AI-edited images are labeled "AI-generated" in the app and carry embedded provenance metadata (IPTC/XMP DigitalSourceType) when saved or shared. AI answers can be inaccurate; always verify important information independently.

Data We Collect

Device Identifier: your device generates a random UUID (via the OS's secure random generator — not a hash derived from any personal identifier) for authentication and quota tracking. If you create an account, this identifier is linked to it. If you make a purchase or watch a rewarded ad, it (or your account identifier) is shared with RevenueCat and Google AdMob to validate purchases and deliver ads. You can use OneAnswerAI anonymously with this identifier alone.

Account Data (optional): If you choose to create an account, we collect and store your email address, an optional display name, and either a securely hashed password or, for social sign-in, an identifier provided by Apple or Google. Account data is used for authentication, account recovery, cross-device sync, and transactional emails (such as email verification and password reset). Creating an account is optional — the app is fully usable in anonymous mode.

Query Text: Your questions are sent to third-party AI providers for processing. The responses may be fused into a single optimized answer. Queries are not permanently stored on our servers beyond cost/usage logging; if you have an account, your full conversations (titles and messages) are synced to our servers for cross-device access.

Media Files: PDF documents and images you submit are sent temporarily to AI providers for analysis. These files are not persisted on our servers and are discarded after processing.

Server-Side Data

To manage quotas and subscriptions, we store the following data on our server (PostgreSQL hosted on Render):

  • Random device UUID (see Device Identifier above)
  • Subscription tier and status
  • Daily query counter
  • Purchased query credits
  • Subscription expiration date
  • Account data (email, optional display name, hashed password or social sign-in identifier) — only if you create an account
  • Conversations (titles and full message text) — only if you create an account
  • AI memory / custom instructions and preferences — only if you create an account
  • Feedback you submit, including screenshots
  • Content reports you submit (excerpt of the reported answer, your note, reason) — guests can submit these too
  • Age range, country, and parental-consent confirmation you declare — only if you create an account
  • An encrypted Sign in with Apple refresh token — only if you sign in with Apple

If you use the app anonymously, no personally identifiable information is stored.

Conversation History & Account Content

If you use the app anonymously, your chat history stays only on your device. If you create an account, your conversations, custom instructions, and preferences are also synced to our servers (see Server-Side Data above) so they are available across your devices.

Advertising & Diagnostics

Advertising (free tier only): The free tier shows rewarded video ads and banner ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob accesses your device's advertising identifier (the Advertising ID on Android and the Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA) on iOS) to deliver and measure ads. On iOS, before any tracking takes place we present Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) prompt: if you allow tracking, AdMob may deliver personalized ads using your advertising identifier; if you decline (or have not granted permission), AdMob serves only non-personalized, contextual ads. Paid subscribers do not see ads. Apple and Google's advertising identifiers are user-resettable and can be limited from your device's privacy settings.

Diagnostics & Crash Reporting: We use Sentry to collect crash reports and basic diagnostic and performance data (such as error logs, device model, and operating system version) so we can detect and fix problems. This data is used solely to improve the app's stability and is not used for advertising.

Data We Do NOT Collect

  • Phone number
  • Precise location data
  • Contacts
  • Payment or financial information (handled entirely by Apple or Google Play)

Third-Party Data Processors (GDPR Art. 28)

Your queries, uploaded media, and certain technical data may be transmitted to the following third-party processors for the purposes described:

AI Processing Providers

  • OpenAI — AI query processing — openai.com/privacy
  • Google Gemini — AI query processing — policies.google.com/privacy
  • Anthropic Claude — AI query processing — anthropic.com/privacy

Data and training: On the free plan, your requests may pass through AI providers whose free tier (e.g. Google Gemini free tier) may use submitted data to improve their models. On paid plans, we use exclusively paid API keys (OpenAI, Anthropic, and Google Gemini on paid API key) under terms that do not allow training on your data. We do not train on your data.

Which specific provider or model is used at any given moment may vary based on your subscription tier and operational factors.

Service Infrastructure

  • Apple App Store: In-app purchases and subscriptions
  • Google Play: In-app purchases and subscriptions (Android) — policies.google.com/privacy
  • Render: Server hosting (PostgreSQL database, API) — render.com/privacy
  • RevenueCat: Subscription validation and IAP receipt processing — revenuecat.com/privacy
  • Google Search (via Gemini grounding): live web results when the web search feature is used — policies.google.com/privacy
  • Sentry: Error tracking and performance monitoring — sentry.io/privacy
  • Resend: Transactional email (verification, password reset) — resend.com/legal/privacy-policy
  • Expo Push Notifications: Push notification delivery — expo.dev/privacy
  • Google AdMob: Rewarded video ads and banner ads (free tier only) — policies.google.com/privacy

International Data Transfers: OneAnswerAI is developed in Italy (EU/EEA). Several processors we use — including OpenAI, Anthropic, Render, RevenueCat, Sentry, Resend, Expo, and Google/AdMob — are based in, or transfer data to, the United States or other countries outside the EEA. Where required, we rely on the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework (for processors certified under it) or the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses to protect data transferred internationally.

In-App Purchases & Subscriptions

OneAnswerAI offers auto-renewable subscriptions and consumable query packs, priced as shown in the App Store or Google Play (prices vary by region and may change). Payments are processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play); we do not collect or store any payment information. You can manage or cancel your subscription in iOS Settings > [Your Name] > Subscriptions, in Google Play > Payments & subscriptions, or from Settings > Manage Subscription inside the app.

Data Security

All communications between the app and our servers use HTTPS encryption. Authentication tokens are stored securely in your device's Keychain.

Data Retention & Account Deletion

Account data is retained for as long as your account remains active. You can permanently delete your account and all associated server-side data at any time from within the app (Settings > Account > Delete Account), or by contacting us at the email below. Deletion is irreversible and permanently removes your profile, conversations, preferences, custom instructions, feedback, content reports, receipts, and quota/subscription records; purchase records associated with your account (transaction identifiers and amounts only, personal identifiers removed) may be retained for accounting purposes.

Your Rights (GDPR/CCPA): if you are in the EEA, UK, or a US state with similar protections, you have the right to access, correct, delete, or export your personal data (see above), and to object to or restrict certain processing. You can exercise these rights from within the app or by contacting us at the email below. If you are in the EU/EEA, you also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority (in Italy, the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali, www.garanteprivacy.it).

Children's Privacy

OneAnswerAI is intended for users 13 years of age or older. If you are in Italy, independent use requires you to be at least 14 years old; a user under 14 in Italy may use the app only with a parent's or legal guardian's confirmed consent. AI image-editing features are restricted to users 18 and older. The app asks for your age range during setup; we do not yet use an OS-level age-verification signal such as Apple's or Google's Age Signals API, so age is self-declared.
